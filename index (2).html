The opportunity

International access. Local ground truth.

Riara Oasis offers overseas investors a route into one of Nairobi’s best-connected residential corridors.

The development sits along Riara Road near The Junction Mall, schools, hospitals and the commercial districts of Kilimani and Lavington. Oscar provides the human layer often missing in an overseas purchase: a clear first conversation, current developer confirmation and a guided path from interest to reservation.
