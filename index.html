The opportunity

International access. Local ground truth.

Riara Oasis offers overseas investors a route into one of Nairobi’s best-connected residential corridors.

The development sits along Riara Road near The Junction Mall, schools, hospitals and the commercial districts of Kilimani and Lavington. Oscar provides a clear first conversation, current developer confirmation and a guided path from interest to reservation.

Your funds do not pass through Oscar.
All reservation and purchase payments are made directly to the developer’s designated account after documentation and terms have been reviewed.
